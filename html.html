About IntelliHack

What: IntelliHack is an intensive, 48-hour competition where university undergraduates collaborate to build innovative solutions using Machine Learning. We provide curated datasets and real-world problems to push the boundaries of what's possible.

Why: Our mission is to foster a community of innovators and problem-solvers. We aim to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry application, giving students a platform to showcase their skills and creativity.

How: Through a series of workshops, mentorship sessions, and challenges, we empower participants to explore advanced topics like RAG models, ethical AI, and large language models, culminating in a final presentation to a panel of industry experts.
